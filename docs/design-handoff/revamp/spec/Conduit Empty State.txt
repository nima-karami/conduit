Shell is 5a/5d verbatim — same pill topbar and labelled Workspace / Board / Canvas switcher, same 266px and 340px panels at 20px radius on the tinted ground; Neon flush with its scanline. Only the panel contents are the empty state.
§7.2 answered rather than restyled: the centre teaches what a session is, then offers the three real routes as a stacked list — one card per row, name, what it does and its shortcut — instead of three tiles competing across a line. A vertical stack reads in one pass and ranks the routes by position, which is the point: New session is the answer most of the time.
The flow is identical in both themes; only the material differs. Same panels, same card order, same copy, same shortcuts — Aero rounds and floats them, Neon squares and notches them. Where the two diverge it is treatment (case, radius, glow), never structure or behaviour.
The alerts chip is suppressed — nothing is running, so the topbar drops it rather than showing "0 need you".
