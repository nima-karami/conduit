Sections and controls come from appearance-sections.ts, not from the cut-off screenshot: Theme & color, Typography (with Density), Background (intensity / surface opacity / blur), Editor & code (wordWrap, surfaceColor, codeOpacity) — all four visible here; Explorer and Session cards continue below the fold, where the bottom mask shows the pane scrolls. The frame is 1060px rather than 820px because sixteen controls do not fit an 820px window: a taller Settings modal is a real layout consequence of the real taxonomy, worth confirming against the running app.
The theme swatch is a window miniature rather than a three-band chip, because radius and the notch are themed axes here and two of the three themes are dark. That needs one added field on ThemeDef — spelled out in the token contract.
Modal geometry is the language's: 20px radius on Aero over a 34% scrim, notched and cyan-edged on Neon, 15px cards, 13px pills and segments throughout.
