48 / 500 nodes · full detail
Nodes are 15px Aero cards with one accent bar, one icon, title, subtitle and ports — a deliberate DOM budget, since §7.6 says 500-node graphs freeze for ~21s. The budget is stated on screen (48 / 500 nodes · full detail), which gives the level-of-detail switch an honest home instead of the app just getting slow.
At 1320px the node gaps are wide enough that four of five edge labels sit on their own line; only the two longest need the shelf above the node row. That is the width paying for itself — at 1060px, three of them collided.
Plan View is dashed amber with a striped bar: an agent-proposed component, using the same vocabulary as the board flag and the review loop.
