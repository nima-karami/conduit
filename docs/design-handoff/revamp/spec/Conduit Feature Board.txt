Board and Canvas are reached from the labelled switcher in the topbar — the same control on every frame, which is the whole §7.8 fix and costs no extra band.
Columns are Aero panels at 20px radius with 15px cards on the tinted ground; Neon runs them flush with 1px #2a2145 dividers and notched cards. Card padding follows --density-bcard-pad; column padding follows --density-bcol-head-pad and --density-board-cols-pad.
Two additions: an amber Agent proposed flag, so the board joins the propose/accept loop rather than being a plain kanban, and WIP counts on Planning and Building — with four agents running, silent accumulation is the failure mode.
